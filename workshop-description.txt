A collection of UI, quality of life and gameplay tweaks, built to my own tastes. Every feature has its own switch, so you can run only the parts you want.

[b]Requires Unity Mod Manager.[/b]

[h2]Settings[/h2]

CataTweaks adds its own tab to the game's Settings screen, in the main menu and in a campaign. Every setting has a tooltip and applies at once, with no restart.

[h2]Quality of life and UI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Remember campaign options between sessions.[/b] Customize Campaign remembers your options and custom faction names when you leave the screen.
[*][b]Hab template naming.[/b] Applying a template renames the hab to Name (Template, Orbit, Body) and uses its icon. Reapplying changes only the brackets. Saving replaces the same-named template.
[*][b]Priority presets overwrite in place.[/b] Saving over a custom preset replaces it, moves its control points to the replacement, and retains default status. Built-in presets are unchanged.
[*][b]Nations keep their preset when priorities change.[/b] When a nation gains or loses a priority, such as a spaceflight program or a military, its preset is applied again within one day. The nation does not change to Custom.
[*][b]One construction queue per station.[/b] All station yards share one ordered queue. Horizontal cards fill the available height at stock width. Currently Building grows with the slots, with progress bars, native hover/selection and a separate well from Queue. Extra slots and waiting jobs scroll inside the card. Free compatible yards work at their own speed. Current jobs keep progress; Clear Queue removes waiting work. Losing a yard preserves waiting jobs. Hab tooltips show each yard's construction or refit. Surface yards keep individual queues. On by default; mutually exclusive with dividers.
[*][b]Automatic shipyard rally (experimental).[/b] A separate option requiring unified station queues. A saved fleet dropdown defaults to None. Completed ships and refits take the earliest affordable rendezvous and merge on arrival. Success is a detailed event without a default popup; insufficient delta-v leaves the ship at the yard with an explanation. Disabling either option stops new rallies; existing orders finish. Later target maneuvers use normal game prompts. Actual transfers, merging and campaign save/load still need in-game verification.
[*][b]Dividers between stations in the ship construction screen.[/b] With shared station queues disabled, the list shows a line between the shipyards of one station and those of the next.
[*][b]Fleet detections name the orbit or body.[/b] A fleet detection notice gives the orbit and the body of the hab. For a landed fleet it gives the body.
[*][b]Councilor portraits do not change with age.[/b] Councilors keep their portraits, icons and videos at every age. Customize offers the same portraits to everyone. Off by default.
[*][b]Nation picker on the nation panel.[/b] The nation name becomes a dropdown of the nations where you hold a control point, in name order, with a previous and a next arrow on each side.
[/list]

[h2]Gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Solar mirrors boost orbital stations.[/b] The solar modules of an orbital station receive the solar mirror bonus. A mirror lights the stations that orbit inward of it. Surface bases do not change. Off by default.
[*][b]Solar mirror orbital boost cap.[/b] The maximum output of the solar modules of a mirror-lit station, from 2 to 8 times their unlit output. The standard limit is 8.
[*][b]Project review favors the expensive project.[/b] Review Failed Projects weights each project by its availability chance multiplied by its research cost. An expensive project is more likely to be selected.
[*][b]Boost priority prefers better launch sites.[/b] Boost can choose the eligible region nearest the equator, excluding occupied regions. On by default, at 50%.
[*][b]Launch site focus.[/b] Chance that Boost chooses the best launch region, from 0% (standard behavior) to 100%. Default 50%.
[*][b]Any faction can go dormant, and can come back.[/b] Allow AI Factions To Be Disabled also applies to the Servants, Protectorate and Humanity First. Only aliens and your faction are spared. Dormant factions return with two control points and no money, orgs or founding income. Off by default.
[*][b]Demand Claim ignores the target's other wars.[/b] A war blocks Demand Claim only if it is a war between the two nations in the claim. The other conditions do not change.
[*][b]Holding a capital borrows that nation's claims.[/b] Holding a dormant nation's original capital without a hostile claim lends you its claims and keeps its nation-gated claim projects available. Losing the capital loses the claims. Hostile borrowed claims need Government and Unity investment. Conquest through them is hostile; unification is peaceful.
[*][b]A nation left on a dormant nation's capital becomes that nation.[/b] If a war leaves a nation without its own capital, and its new capital is the original capital of a dormant nation that claims it, the dormant nation comes back with all its regions once there is peace. In Broken Earth, a Westralia that loses Perth and keeps Java becomes Indonesia.
[*][b]Repeatable management project scaling.[/b] Management, Audience, Commercial and Operations Research gain a percentage of the base reward per prior completion. Set 0% for standard flat rewards.
[*][b]Control points can be traded.[/b] A diplomacy tab lists control points by nation. The AI prices them and offers abandoned seats. Alien factions and alien nations are excluded. On by default.
[*][b]Control point base value.[/b] How much the AI weights a control point against the other items on the table, from 0.5x to 5x.
[*][b]Suppressed control points can be traded.[/b] Crackdown moves with a traded seat and retains its remaining time. Trading cannot clear it. Off by default.
[*][b]Purging a friendly control point does not break the pact.[/b] Purging a suppressed seat belonging to a faction with a non-aggression pact or truce causes no anger or pact break. Off by default.
[*][b]Show Triggered Projects keeps achievements.[/b] The Show Triggered Projects campaign option does not mark a campaign as custom difficulty, so the Normal, Veteran and Brutal victory achievements stay available. On by default.
[*][b]Warn before a mission breaks a pact.[/b] A mission against a faction that has a non-aggression pact or a truce with you marks the selected target. The game asks you to confirm before it assigns the councilor. On by default.
[/list]

[h2]Campaign options[/h2]
Both of these options are in the 'Customize Campaign' dialogue, and are lightly tested. Neither option has been played through a full campaign.
[list]
[*][b]Spy slots become councilor slots.[/b] The eight slots on the council screen become one pool, so an unused spy slot is another councilor. With no spies you have eight councilors, with two spies six. Seats seven and eight are unlocked by two new projects after Covert Operations. The AI uses the same caps.
[*][b]Restored Empires claims.[/b] Claim chains and four projects for old powers: The Sun Never Sets, expanded Commonwealth Restored, New Liberia, and Rome's Mare Nostrum and Imperium Sine Fine (Broken Earth only). Claims on territories that fought for independence are hostile.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]

[b]AI was used to create this mod, including most of the text of this post and most text in the mod. The mod is localized (by machine) in every language TI ships. If that bothers you, don't feel compelled to use it. I made it for myself, and was asked to post it.[/b]

A save made with this mod still loads without it.

Works alongside Expanded Council (the spy slot option is turned off while it is installed), Alien Threat Analysis and Cooperative Missions. None of them is required.
