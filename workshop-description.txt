[h1]CataTweaks[/h1]

A collection of UI, quality of life and gameplay tweaks, built to my own tastes. Every feature has its own switch, so you can run only the parts you want.

[b]Requires Unity Mod Manager.[/b] Get it from nexusmods.com/site/mods/21, run it, pick Terra Invicta, set the game folder, choose DoorstopProxy, and click Install. The included README has the same steps.

[h2]Settings[/h2]

CataTweaks adds its own tab to the game's Settings screen, in the main menu and in a loaded campaign. Every setting has a tooltip. Changes apply the moment you click them, with no restart and no reload.

Two settings belong to the campaign rather than to you. They sit at the bottom of New Game / Customize Campaign and are stored in the save, so a campaign always plays the way it was started.

[h2]Quality of life and UI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Campaign options persist between sessions.[/b] Customize Campaign opens with your last setup instead of the defaults, and saves when you leave the screen rather than only when you launch. Custom faction names are remembered per faction.
[*][b]Hab templates.[/b] Applying a template names the hab "Name (Template, Orbit, Body)" and applies the template's map icon. Saving a hab as a template overwrites the previous version in place. A template that changes nothing can still be applied, so you can rename a hab on its own.
[*][b]Priority presets overwrite in place.[/b] Saving over an existing preset name replaces it, and control points using the old version follow it, so countries stop flipping to Custom.
[*][b]Nations keep their preset when their priorities change[/b], such as when they gain a spaceflight program or a military.
[*][b]Dividers between stations[/b] in the ship construction list.
[*][b]Fleet detection notices name the orbit or body[/b], not just the hab.
[/list]

[h2]Gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Solar mirrors boost orbital stations.[/b] The game builds the entire mirror mechanism and then spends it on surface bases only. Now stations benefit too, with a direction rule: a mirror lights targets orbiting inward of it. Surface bases are unchanged. Off by default.
[*][b]Solar mirror boost cap.[/b] Maximum output of a mirror-lit station, from 2x to 8x its unlit output. 8 is the vanilla ceiling.
[*][b]Project review favors the expensive project.[/b] Review Failed Projects divides by research cost, so a cheap throwaway beats an expensive drive by a wide margin. This multiplies instead.
[*][b]Demand Claim ignores the target's other wars.[/b] The game asks whether the target is at war at all, not whether it is at war with you, so one endless alien war freezes peaceful border changes for the rest of the campaign.
[*][b]Holding a capital borrows that nation's claims.[/b] Absorbing a nation moves everything except its claims, which is what forces unification to be worked from the outside in. While you hold a dormant nation's original capital with a non-hostile claim on it, its claims are yours to use. Lose the capital and they go with it. Hostile claims stay hostile, and living nations are excluded.
[*][b]Repeatable management project scaling.[/b] Management, Audience, Commercial and Operations Research cost more on each repeat but pay the same flat amount. Each repeat now pays more in step with its cost. Set it to 0% for vanilla.
[/list]

[h2]Campaign options[/h2]

[i]Both of these are lightly tested. They load and behave as described, but neither has been played through a campaign. Turn them off if you want a settled game.[/i]
[list]
[*][b]Spy slots become councilor slots.[/b] The council screen has eight slots: six councilors and two for councilors you turned elsewhere. This makes them one pool, so an unused spy slot is another councilor. No spies seats eight, two spies seats six. Seats seven and eight are unlocked by two new projects after Covert Operations. The AI plays it too, and will trade its spies for councilors.
[*][b]Restored Empires claims.[/b] Claim chains and four projects that give old powers a route back to the map. The Sun Never Sets takes Britain past the settler dominions, and Commonwealth Restored gains Delhi, Calcutta and Ireland. New Liberia opens the West African coast for the Dominion of America, which also claims Jamaica. Mare Nostrum and Imperium Sine Fine give Rome the Mediterranean, the eastern roads and both American seaboards, in Broken Earth only. Claims on territories that fought their way out are hostile.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]

A save made with this mod still loads without it.

Restored Empires used to be a separate mod and is now part of this one. If you still have it installed, remove it, or its claim rows will be duplicated.
